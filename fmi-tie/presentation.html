Why it matters

Parking Is Broken

Cities lose billions in productivity annually — and the root cause is invisible.

🚗
30% of Traffic

Up to 30% of urban traffic at any given moment is cars circling looking for a parking spot — adding to congestion with zero value.

⛽
Wasted Fuel & Emissions

Unnecessary low-speed driving burns fuel and raises CO₂ emissions citywide — a direct environmental and economic cost.

😤
Driver Frustration

Stress, lost time, and late arrivals every single day. Existing apps show static paid lots — they don't see the street in real time.

Introducing SpotHawk

Drone-Powered Parking Intelligence

SpotHawk turns the sky into your parking sensor.

🚁
Drone Fleet

Autonomous drones continuously patrol urban zones, capturing live aerial footage of every street and lot.

👁️
Computer Vision

AI processes live footage in real time, detecting exactly which spots are free or occupied — with high accuracy.

📱
Mobile App

Drivers see a live parking map and get routed to the nearest free spot instantly — from launch to navigation in under 3 taps.

What makes us different

Competitive Advantage

Existing Solutions
🗺️ Google Maps / ParkMobile

Show pre-listed paid lots only. Static data. Don't see streets or informal zones. No real-time detection.

📡 Ground Sensor Systems (Siemens, Kapsch)

Accurate but cost tens of thousands per street to install. Take years to deploy. Cannot cover an entire city affordably.

❌ The Gap

No solution today provides full-city, real-time, street-level parking visibility at a reasonable deployment cost.

SpotHawk Advantage
🌍 Full City Surface Coverage

Streets, lots, and informal zones — all visible. Not just pre-registered paid spots.

⚡ Zero Ground Infrastructure

No sensors to install. No permits per street. Deploy a city in days, not years.

🔁 Redeployable in Hours

Drone routes can shift to new zones instantly as demand changes — no hardware relocation needed.

📸 Real-Time, Not Pre-Listed

Live aerial detection — not a database of lots. We see what's actually happening on the street right now.
