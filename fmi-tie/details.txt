0.02

--- Segments ---
Urban drivers/commuters – People who regularly need parking in busy city centers; want fast, reliable info on available spots.
Visitors and occasional drivers – Tourists, shoppers, or event-goers unfamiliar with local parking patterns; need easy guidance to free spaces.
City municipalities or administrations – Interested in reducing congestion, optimizing parking infrastructure, and monitoring urban mobility.

--- Value ---
Real-time parking availability: Shows which parking spots are free right now.
Predictive guidance: Uses historical data to estimate where spots are likely to open soon.
Time and stress savings: Reduces the time spent searching for parking in busy areas.
Traffic and fuel efficiency: Helps cut unnecessary driving, lowering congestion and emissions.
Seamless access: Available through mobile app for convenience.
Operational insights for businesses/cities: Lets parking operators and municipalities monitor usage and optimize space management.

--- Channels ---
Mobile app / navigation integration – primary way drivers see available parking spots.
Push notifications / alerts – instant updates when a preferred area has free spaces.
Website / city portal – optional access for planning trips in advance.
Partnerships with rideshare / mapping services – expand reach by integrating into apps people already use.

--- Relationships ---
Self-service & automated guidance: Users access real-time parking info through the app or navigation system with minimal human intervention.
Personalized experience: Predictive recommendations based on user location, parking history, and preferences.
Proactive notifications: Alerts for nearby free spaces or high-probability availability zones.
Trust & reliability: Consistently updated data to build confidence in the system.

--- Activities ---
Drone Operations & Maintenance – patrolling urban areas, keeping drones functional and compliant.
Data Capture & Processing – collecting aerial images and running computer vision algorithms.
Real-Time Parking Map Updates – continuously integrating drone data into the app and predictive model.
Predictive Analytics – refining historical data to forecast parking availability.
App & Platform Management – ensuring mobile interface is accurate, responsive, and user-friendly.
Customer Support – assisting users, handling issues, and maintaining engagement.

--- Resources ---
Autonomous drones – hardware capable of safe urban flight and image capture.
Computer vision software – algorithms to detect parking occupancy accurately.
Data infrastructure – cloud storage, real-time processing, and mapping platforms.
Mobile app / navigation interface – for delivering parking info to users.
Skilled team – engineers for drones, AI, and software maintenance.
Regulatory approvals / permits – legal clearance for urban drone operations.

--- Partners ---
Drone manufacturers & suppliers – provide reliable hardware and maintenance support.
Computer vision/AI tech providers – supply software, models, or APIs for image analysis.
City authorities & parking operators – grant permissions for aerial surveillance and access to municipal parking data.
Telecom & cloud service providers – enable real-time data transmission, storage, and processing.
Mapping/navigation platforms – provide geographical data

--- Revenue ---
Subscription fees: Drivers pay a monthly/yearly fee for real-time access to parking availability and predictive analytics.
Pay-per-use: Occasional users can pay for single-session access through the app.
B2B partnerships: Parking lot operators, municipalities, and delivery/logistics companies pay for integration of data to optimize operations.
Advertising & premium placement: Local businesses or services can sponsor highlighted parking zones or promoted areas in the app.

--- Costs ---
Drone fleet costs – purchase, maintenance, batteries, and replacement cycles.
Software and AI – development, updates, cloud computing, and computer vision processing.
Data infrastructure – servers, storage, and network bandwidth for real-time maps.
Personnel – engineers, drone operators, and support staff.
Regulatory and insurance – permits for drone flights, liability coverage, compliance fees.
Marketing & customer support – app promotion, customer service, and retention efforts.
